    I am a Habilitated Associate Professor of Software Engineering at the National School of Computer Science and Systems Analysis (ENSIAS), Mohammed V University in Rabat, where I am the Head of the Software Engineering Department.

    My research activities lie in Software and Systems Engineering, with a focus on Model-Driven Engineering. With my Ph.D. students, I develop languages, models and tools that make the design, deployment and operation of software-intensive systems more automated and more reliable, with applications to domains such as agriculture, health and industry.

    In most of my current research, I investigate:

    • Model-Driven (Software and Systems) Engineering
    • Domain-Specific (Modeling) Languages
    • AI-assisted Software Engineering
    • Dev(Sec)Ops
    • Self-Adaptive Systems
    • Digital Twins
    • ICT for Sustainability

    My publications are listed on the Publications page and on Google Scholar. More information in my complete bio.
