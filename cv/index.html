    Short version

    Mahmoud El Hamlaoui is a Habilitated Associate Professor of Software Engineering at the National School of Computer Science and Systems Analysis (ENSIAS), Mohammed V University in Rabat, where he has taught since 2016. He received his Ph.D. in Software Engineering in 2015 under a joint supervision between the University of Toulouse Jean Jaurès and Mohammed V University in Rabat, after a State Engineering Degree in Software Engineering from ENSIAS in 2010. Before joining ENSIAS, he was a temporary lecturer and researcher in Toulouse and an R&D engineer at Netfective Technology. His research interests include Model-Driven Engineering, Systems and Software Engineering, Digital Twins, AI-assisted Software Engineering and ICT for Sustainability.
